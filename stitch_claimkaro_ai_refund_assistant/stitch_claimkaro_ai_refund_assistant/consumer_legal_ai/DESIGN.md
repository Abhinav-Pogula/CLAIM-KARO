## Brand & Style

This design system powers an Indian consumer technology and automated legal grievance platform. It is engineered to dismantle the intimidation of corporate arbitration, consumer forums, and e-commerce dispute resolutions into an accessible, empowering self-serve experience.

The design philosophy unites modern fintech clarity with legal transparency:
- **Atmosphere:** Clean, airy, structured, and modern card-first architecture. It prioritizes psychological relief for consumers experiencing financial friction or purchase frustration.
- **AI Transparency:** Automated intelligence is surfaced through purposeful visual accents—represented by indigo-to-violet linear transitions—demystifying legal parsing, ticket drafting, and evidence classification without feeling alien or opaque.
- **Indian Consumer Context:** Optimized for varied mobile viewport constraints, fluctuating connectivity, multi-source evidence uploading (voice notes, UPI screenshots, invoice PDFs), and rupee-denominated financial restitution.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate foundation of consumer trust, legal authority, and visual warmth.

### Functional Palette
- **Canvas Background:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) establishes an airy, low-glare surface.
- **Card & Sheet Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) elevated against the canvas.
- **Primary Indigo:** `#4F46E5` (Base Interactive), `#4338CA` (Hover / Pressed), `#EEF2FF` (Subtle Tint / Background Layer).
- **Secondary Violet:** `#7C3AED` drives AI procedural indicators, analysis banners, and automated grievance summaries.
- **Accent Saffron:** `#F59E0B` introduces cultural vibrancy, priority markers, and urgent legal action highlights.

### Text Contrast Tiers
- **Heading:** `#0F172A` (Slate 900) — High-contrast legibility for titles, claims, and settlement values.
- **Body & Controls:** `#334155` (Slate 700) — Calibrated reading contrast for dispute narratives and legal terms.
- **Muted & Captions:** `#64748B` (Slate 500) — De-emphasized metadata, timestamps, and secondary labels.

### Feedback & Semantic Validation
- **Success:** `#16A34A` | Tint: `#DCFCE7` (Refund processed, case resolved, settlement approved).
- **Warning:** `#D97706` | Tint: `#FEF3C7` (Response overdue, counter-offer received, document verification required).
- **Danger:** `#DC2626` | Tint: `#FEE2E2` (Merchant rejection, escalated dispute, missing evidentiary criteria).

### Evidence Categorization Tokens
- **Photo Evidence:** `#0EA5E9` (Sky 500) — Product defects, packaging tears, courier seals.
- **Voice Memos:** `#8B5CF6` (Violet 500) — WhatsApp call records, recorded merchant interactions.
- **Invoices & Bills:** `#10B981` (Emerald 500) — Tax invoices, GST receipts, payment ledger slips.
- **System / Edited:** `#475569` (Slate 600) — Machine-redacted inputs, merchant logs, manual audits.

### Visual Effects
- **AI Processing Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #4F46E5 0%, #7C3AED 100%)` for automated generation buttons, scanning meters, and AI assistant badges.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional division of labor across three complementary typefaces:

1. **Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric yet warm proportions impart friendly authority. Generous apertures keep screen titles clear and welcoming to users dealing with high-stress disputes.
2. **Body & UI Controls (Inter):** High x-height, neutral grotesk architecture that maintains optimal legibility across varying reading conditions, formal legal notices, and complex timelines.
3. **Identifiers & Monetary (JetBrains Mono):** Dedicated to technical data points: Claim tracking IDs, court filing numbers, GSTIN identifiers, and Indian Rupee values (`₹12,499.00`). Monospaced alignment prevents tabular jitter during active balance recalibrations.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8pt base grid dictates vertical and horizontal rhythm, adjusted to a 4pt sub-grid for compact meta-labels and tabular rows.

- **Mobile (Default):** Fluid single-column layout with a 16px outer safety gutter. Edge-to-edge modular cards maintain maximum readable surface area on small displays.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** Fluid 6-column system with 24px margins. Allows two-column splits between evidence upload panels and running claim summaries.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Structured 12-column fixed-max grid centered at `1200px` max-width. A persistent 4-column side module handles live dispute status, legal counsel AI metrics, and payout calculations, while the 8-column canvas drives active litigation drafting.
- **Component Vertical Cadence:** Form field groups maintain 16px margins; standalone cards and content chunks separate at 24px intervals.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes ultra-soft ambient light to sustain a clean, unencumbered visual plane. Hard drop shadows are avoided in favor of diffuse ambient occlusions.

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Surface `#FFFFFF` resting on `#F8FAFC` without shadow, bounded by a fine outline `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`. Used for secondary evidence slots and inactive steps.
- **Level 1 (Card Default):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 24px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` combined with a subtle structural perimeter `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Used for primary case cards, refund calculation cards, and timeline checkpoints.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover):** `box-shadow: 0 8px 32px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`, lifting cards during active interaction, drag-and-drop file ingestion, or list focus.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Action Sheets & Bottom Trays):** `box-shadow: 0 16px 48px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)` with a tinted backdrop overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.40)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).
- **AI Elevation Accent:** For AI-generated legal notices and auto-extracted entity cards, a dual-layer glow is applied: `0 4px 24px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.08), 0 1px 2px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs explicit, strictly disciplined geometry tailored to element roles:

- **Cards & Surfaces:** Rigorous `16px` border radius (`rounded-lg` level). Provides a soft, protective feel without sacrificing structural alignment.
- **Buttons, Controls & Input Fields:** Standardized `12px` border radius. Strikes a balance between tap-target approachability and utilitarian precision.
- **Pills, Badges & Chips:** Completely circularized `999px` capsule radius. Enforces strong visual contrast against rectangular card surfaces.
- **Evidence Thumbnails & Media Insets:** `8px` corner radius to nest proportionally inside 16px parent cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Indigo (`#4F46E5`), text `#FFFFFF`, radius `12px`, height `48px` (mobile primary) or `40px` (desktop), padding `0 20px`. Hover: `#4338CA`. Active state: subtle scale down `0.98`.
- **AI Action Variant:** Background `linear-gradient(135deg, #4F46E5 0%, #7C3AED 100%)`, text `#FFFFFF`, icon slot prepended with sparkle glyph. Used exclusively for operations like "Auto-Draft Notice" or "Analyze Invoice".
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent base, border `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`, text `#0F172A`, hover background `#F1F5F9`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#DC2626`, hover background `#FCA5A5` at 40% opacity.

### Cards
- **Base Style:** Background `#FFFFFF`, corner radius `16px`, shadow `0 4px 24px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, padding `20px` mobile, `24px` desktop.
- **Evidence Upload Card:** Dotted border `1.5px dashed #CBD5E1`, background `#F8FAFC`, transition to `#EEF2FF` on drag-over, nested status tags using specific source evidence colors.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Height `24px`, radius `999px`, font `pill-tag`, padding `0 10px`. 
  - *Refund Approved:* `#DCFCE7` text `#16A34A`.
  - *Under AI Audit:* `#EEF2FF` text `#4F46E5`.
  - *Escalated to NCH:* `#FEF3C7` text `#D97706`.
- **Evidence Source Badges:** Pill shape with a leading 6px colored indicator dot:
  - *Photo:* Tint background `#E0F2FE`, dot `#0EA5E9`, text `#0369A1`.
  - *Voice Note:* Tint background `#EDE9FE`, dot `#8B5CF6`, text `#6D28D9`.
  - *Invoice/PDF:* Tint background `#D1FAE5`, dot `#10B981`, text `#047857`.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Container:** Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `12px`, font `body-md`. 
- **Focus State:** Border color `#4F46E5`, ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.12)`.
- **Monetary Inputs:** Prepended with fixed currency indicator `₹` set in `JetBrains Mono` medium `#64748B`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Radio Buttons:** `20px` circle, border `2px solid #CBD5E1`, selected background `#4F46E5` containing an inner `6px` white circle.
- **Checkboxes:** `20px` square, `6px` border radius, filled `#4F46E5` on selection with an inset white checkmark.

### Specialized Claim Components
- **Litigation Timeline Tracker:** Vertical or horizontal path connecting `12px` circular nodes. Active legal step highlighted with an outer ring in `#EEF2FF` containing a `#4F46E5` center; completed steps in `#16A34A`.
- **Settlement Calculator Bar:** Dual-tone progress bar computing total claimed vs. merchant-offered amounts, using `JetBrains Mono` for live numerical counter readouts.
